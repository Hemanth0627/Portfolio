ESP32-EdgeHub

A Flask dashboard for controlling an ESP32 in plain English. It switches at runtime between Google Gemini and a local Qwen3 4B model on Ollama, with an Auto mode that falls back to the local model. A live Three.js model of the board mirrors GPIO 2, and it adds PWM and ADC control, OTA firmware updates, telemetry history and a safe-listed pin registry.

  • ESP32
  • Flask
  • Gemini · Ollama
  • Three.js
  • OTA

Core competencies

Power electronics & converter design

Analysing and designing high-gain DC-DC topologies from first principles. At NIT Calicut I derived steady-state expressions for voltage gain, capacitor voltages, inductor ripple and device stress for a quadratic-boost converter with a Quasi-Z-Source network, then sized L and C from ripple constraints while keeping device stress below 90% of output.

  • DC-DC converters
  • Quasi-Z-Source
  • Component sizing
  • Device stress analysis
Renewable energy integration

Stepping low PV-level voltages up to DC-bus levels efficiently. My converter design reached 10 V to 344 V at 70% duty cycle, around 10× a conventional boost at the same duty, which suits interfacing solar and other low-voltage sources to grid-tied systems.

  • Solar PV interfacing
  • High step-up gain
  • DC bus design
Circuit simulation

Building and validating models in MATLAB/Simulink and LTspice, comparing simulated waveforms against analytical results to confirm a design before hardware. At Tryneutera Electronics I use MATLAB to simulate phased-array antennas for SATCOM beam steering, and I document each workflow in a technical report.

  • MATLAB/Simulink
  • LTspice
  • Waveform validation
  • Technical reporting
Embedded systems

These boards run my smart blind stick, OLED animation player, ultrasonic radar and ESP32-EdgeHub, and I prototype on breadboards and debug with an oscilloscope and function generator.
